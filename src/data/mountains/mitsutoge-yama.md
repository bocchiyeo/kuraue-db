# Mt. Mitsutoge (三ッ峠山)

**Elevation:** 1,785 m  
**Location:** Yamanashi  
**Anime appearance:** Season 2 Ep 2–4

The friends camp together before hiking Mitsutoge, where Aoi gets a memorable close-up view of Mt. Fuji. As one of the group's first longer mountain outings, it is a big step—and a special one to share.
