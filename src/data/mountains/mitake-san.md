# Mt. Mitake (御岳山)

**Elevation:** 929 m  
**Location:** Ome, Tokyo  
**Anime appearance:** *Next Summit*, episode 8

Aoi brings her friends to explore Mitake's shrine and mountain-side power spots, making for an outing with plenty to discover. The adventure can continue along the ridge toward Mt. Hinode.
