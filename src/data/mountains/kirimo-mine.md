# Mt. Kirimo (霧藻ヶ峰)

**Elevation:** 1,523 m  
**Location:** Chichibu, Saitama  
**Anime appearance:** *Next Summit*, episode 11 route

The route from Mitsumine toward Mt. Kumotori passes over Kirimo, adding another high point to the group's demanding mountain journey. It is a route-based identification rather than a summit specifically named in the episode synopsis.
