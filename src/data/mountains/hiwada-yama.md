# Mt. Hiwada (日和田山)

**Elevation:** 305 m  
**Location:** Hidaka, Saitama  
**Anime appearance:** *Next Summit*, episode 7

The group heads out before dawn with one special goal: watch the first sunrise of the year from the trail. Hiwada sets the scene for a memorable start to the day; the same episode also features a separate Takao hike.
