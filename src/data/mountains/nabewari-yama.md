# Mt. Nabewari (鍋割山)

**Elevation:** 1,272 m  
**Location:** Kanagawa  
**Anime appearance:** *Next Summit*, episode 10

Hinata leads Aoi and Kokona up to Nabewari's mountain hut, where its famous nabeyaki udon makes a tempting trail reward. Episode-location guides identify this outing; the official episode synopsis focuses on the other hike at Tenran.
