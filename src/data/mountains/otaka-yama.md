# Mt. Otaka (大高山)

**Elevation:** 493 m  
**Location:** Hanno, Saitama  
**Anime appearance:** Season 3, episode 3

Aoi sets out to cross the Hanno Alps solo, then meets Kokona at Tenkaku and continues along the route with her. Otaka is one of the named summits on their rolling traverse, rather than a separate trip in the story.
