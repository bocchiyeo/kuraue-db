# Mt. Tenkaku (天覚山)

**Elevation:** about 445 m  
**Location:** Hanno, Saitama  
**Anime appearance:** Season 3, episode 3

Aoi starts her Hanno Alps traverse alone and quickly finds the rolling trail testing her pace. At Tenkaku, she meets Kokona, and the two continue along the route together.
