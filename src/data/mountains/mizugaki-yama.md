# Mt. Mizugaki (瑞牆山)

**Elevation:** 2,230 m  
**Location:** Yamanashi  
**Anime appearance:** Season 3, episodes 11–12

Aoi, Hinata, Kokona, and Kaede camp overnight before setting out for Mizugaki's rugged granite peak. Its striking rocky landscape makes this a big step beyond the group's local hikes.
