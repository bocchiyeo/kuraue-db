# Mt. Tanigawa (谷川岳)

**Elevation:** 1,977 m (Oki-no-Mimi)  
**Location:** Gunma–Niigata  
**Anime appearance:** Season 2 Ep 21–23

The group takes on Tanigawa's dramatic alpine terrain and its two main summits. Along the way, the friends meet Honoka, whose connection with them continues long after the hike.

The two summits are Toma-no-Mimi (1,963 m) and Oki-no-Mimi (1,977 m).
