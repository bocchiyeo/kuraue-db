# Mt. Atago, Tokyo (愛宕山)

**Elevation:** about 26 m  
**Location:** Minato, Tokyo  
**Anime appearance:** *Next Summit*, episode 3

Hinata turns a city outing into a mini-climb, leading Aoi up the steep shrine steps of Atago. It is a compact but memorable way to reach Tokyo's natural high point within the 23 wards.
