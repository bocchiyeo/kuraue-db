# Mt. Choshichiro (長七郎山)

**Elevation:** about 1,579 m  
**Location:** Akagi, Gunma  
**Anime appearance:** *Next Summit*, episode 8

The group straps on snowshoes for a winter outing on Akagi, with Choshichiro as their summit. It is a fresh snowy adventure, distinct from the earlier Akagi hike to Jizo-dake.
