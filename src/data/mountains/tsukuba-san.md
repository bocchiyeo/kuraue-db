# Mt. Tsukuba (筑波山)

**Elevation:** 877 m  
**Location:** Ibaraki  
**Anime appearance:** Season 3, episode 1

Aoi dreams up a night hike to Tsukuba as a special outing with Hinata. Its twin summits and after-dark approach make this a fresh, atmospheric change from their usual daytime hikes.
