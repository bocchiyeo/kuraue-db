# Mt. Buko (武甲山)

**Elevation:** 1,304 m  
**Location:** Chichibu, Saitama  
**Anime appearance:** *Next Summit*, episode 5

Aoi and Hinata team up with Aoi's mother for a challenging training hike up Buko. At 1,304 m, it is one of the larger Saitama peaks featured in the series—and a memorable step up for the group.
