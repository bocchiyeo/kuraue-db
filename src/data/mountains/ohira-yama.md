# Mt. Ohira (太平山)

**Elevation:** 159 m  
**Location:** Kamakura, Kanagawa  
**Anime appearance:** *Next Summit*, episode 9

A family outing follows the Kamakura Alps/Tenen trail, with Ohira as one of the route's gentle summit stops. It is a lovely way to enjoy Kamakura's hills together; the official synopsis describes the walk without naming the peak.
