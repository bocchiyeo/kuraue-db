# Mt. Kumotori (雲取山)

**Elevation:** 2,017 m  
**Location:** Tokyo–Saitama–Yamanashi  
**Anime appearance:** *Next Summit*, episode 11

Aoi and her friends push through rainy conditions on a demanding climb, then camp near the summit. Kumotori becomes a true team challenge—and an important training trip as Aoi prepares for Fuji.
