# Mt. Tenran (天覧山)

**Elevation:** 197 m  
**Location:** Hanno, Saitama  
**Anime appearances:** Season 1, episode 3; Season 2 Ep 12; Season 3, episodes 2 and 13; *Next Summit*, episodes 5 and 10

Tenran is one of the group's most familiar local hills, and Aoi keeps finding reasons to return. She trains here for another Fuji attempt, meets Kokona on the trail, and comes back for more hikes with friends and classmates.

The anime names Tenran in Season 3, episode 13, and Season 4, episode 10. Its many appearances make this familiar Hanno hill a recurring part of Aoi's growing hiking story.
