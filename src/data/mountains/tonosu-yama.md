# Mt. Tonosu (多峯主山)

**Elevation:** 271 m  
**Location:** Hanno, Saitama  
**Anime appearance:** Season 2 Ep 12

After climbing Tenran, Aoi and Hinata keep their day on the trail going with a walk to nearby Tonosu. Pairing the two local summits makes for a relaxed, rewarding Hanno outing.
