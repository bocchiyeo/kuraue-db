# Mt. Takao (高尾山)

**Elevation:** 599 m  
**Location:** Hachioji, Tokyo  
**Anime appearances:** Season 1, episodes 8–10; Season 4, episode 7

Aoi and Hinata take on Takao together and meet Kokona while descending, a chance encounter that begins a lasting friendship. In *Next Summit*, Aoi returns with Hinata and classmates for another memorable hike.
