# Mt. Kinpu (金峰山)

**Elevation:** 2,599 m  
**Location:** Yamanashi–Nagano  
**Anime appearance:** Season 3, episodes 11–12

After camping overnight at Mizugaki, the group gets an early start for Kinpu—the next major climb and a high, rewarding summit. The journey brings together the excitement of a night outdoors and the challenge of a full mountain day.
