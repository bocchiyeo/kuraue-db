# Mt. Hinode (日の出山)

**Elevation:** 902 m  
**Location:** Tokyo  
**Anime appearance:** *Next Summit*, episode 8

The group keeps the adventure going beyond Mt. Mitake, following the ridge to Hinode. This rewarding continuation turns a shrine visit into a longer day on the trail.
