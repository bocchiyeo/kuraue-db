# Mt. Kuruma (車山)

**Elevation:** 1,925 m  
**Location:** Kirigamine, Nagano  
**Anime appearance:** Season 2 Ep 14

Aoi explores the open highlands around Mt. Kuruma on a family outing. The broad, airy landscape offers a refreshing change of pace from the forest trails near Hanno.
