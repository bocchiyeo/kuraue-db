# Mt. Fuji (富士山)

**Elevation:** 3,776 m  
**Location:** Shizuoka–Yamanashi  
**Anime appearances:** Season 2 Ep 8–11; *Next Summit*, episodes 11–12

Fuji is Aoi’s long-held goal, and reaching for it becomes one of her biggest challenges. Altitude sickness forces her to turn back on her first attempt while Hinata and Kokona continue to the summit. Aoi keeps training and later returns with her friends for another try.

Climbing conditions are high-alpine and the mountain should only be climbed on an official route during the published climbing season.
