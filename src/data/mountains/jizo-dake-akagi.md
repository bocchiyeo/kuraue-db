# Mt. Jizo, Akagi (地蔵岳)

**Elevation:** about 1,674 m  
**Location:** Akagi, Gunma  
**Anime appearance:** Season 3, episodes 8–9

With Aoi away on another trip, Hinata and Kokona make their own adventure of it and climb Akagi. Episode-location guides identify their summit as Jizo-dake, a high point with sweeping mountain scenery.
