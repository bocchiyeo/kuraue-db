# Kurodake

Kurodake is a classic Hokkaido climb with a mellow challenge and a layered landscape that shifts from forest to exposed ridge. The climb rewards hikers with crisp air and a clear view of the neighboring peaks.

## Why Hikers Love It

- Easy-to-follow trail signs
- Strong panoramic viewpoints from the ridge
- A manageable climb that still feels rewarding

## Route Advice

Bring a light layer even on warmer mornings. The ridge can cool quickly once the sun dips and the wind picks up.

### Suggested Schedule

1. Start early and move at a relaxed pace.
2. Stop at the lookout for photos and rest.
3. Finish with a gentle descent before the afternoon heat.

## Scenic Summary

This mountain is ideal for a day trip when you want mountain views without a full expedition feeling.
